Move-Out Cleaning Checklist

Not what we clean — what makes the visit go smoothly. A few minutes of prep usually means a faster, better clean.

Before we arrive

  • Move out personal belongings where you can — empty rooms clean far better
  • Empty the cabinets if you want inside-cabinet cleaning
  • Empty and defrost the fridge if you want the inside done
  • Take the last of the garbage and recycling out if possible
  • Make sure we can actually get in — keys, lockbox, buzzer code or someone on site
  • Confirm the power and water are still on

Tell us when you request the quote

  • Bedrooms and bathrooms, and roughly how big the place is
  • Your move-out date and when the property has to be ready
  • Any heavy buildup or unusual conditions, so the quote is realistic
  • Priority areas — the parts you most want done properly
  • Whether you want the oven, fridge or cabinets included
  • A photo or two if anything is easier to show than describe

The more accurate the description, the more accurate the quote — and the less chance of the scope changing on the day.

Move-Out Cleaning FAQ

What is included in a move-out cleaning?

A move-out clean covers the kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas — reachable surfaces, counters, sinks, fixtures, appliance exteriors, mirrors, and floors throughout. Because the property is usually empty, more surfaces are reachable than in a regular clean. Detail work such as inside the oven, inside the fridge and inside cabinets is priced as an add-on rather than included by default. The exact scope is confirmed when we quote.

How much does move-out cleaning cost in Kamloops?

The cost depends on factors such as the size of the property, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, condition, and whether additional detail cleaning is requested. Use our estimator for a quick ballpark price, or request a quote for your specific property.

Can I book a move-out cleaning for today or tomorrow?

Often, yes. Move dates shift constantly, so short-notice requests are normal here. Same-day and next-day appointments may be available depending on the schedule that day, so calling (250) 879-2425 is the fastest way to find out. We can’t promise every request, but you’ll get a straight answer quickly either way.

Do you clean apartments and condos?

Yes — apartments, condos, townhouses and rental houses, as well as detached homes. Smaller units generally take less time and are priced accordingly; the estimator on this page starts at studio and one-bedroom sizes.

Do you clean inside ovens and refrigerators?

Yes, both are available as add-ons. They are priced separately from the standard move-out scope rather than included by default — you can see the current add-on prices in the estimator on this page, or just ask for them when you request your quote.

Do you clean inside cabinets?

Inside cabinets is available as an add-on as well. It works best when the cabinets are already empty, so if you want it included, clear them before the visit and mention it when you request the quote.

How long does a move-out cleaning take?

It depends on the size of the property, its condition and the agreed scope. An empty one-bedroom in reasonable shape is a much shorter visit than a large home with heavy buildup. We’ll give you a realistic time estimate along with the price when the quote is confirmed.

Do I need to remove everything before the cleaners arrive?

Ideally, yes — a move-out clean works best on an empty property, because every surface is reachable and nothing has to be worked around. If some items will still be there, tell us when you request the quote so the scope and timing account for it.

Can landlords or property managers book the service?

Yes. Landlords and property managers can book directly — turnover cleans between tenants are a core part of what Urgent Clean does. Send the property details and the date it needs to be ready, and we’ll confirm scope, availability and pricing. Several units can be quoted at once.

Does move-out cleaning guarantee my damage deposit back?

No. No cleaning company can honestly guarantee that. Whether a deposit is returned is decided by your landlord or property manager based on their inspection and the overall condition of the property — including things cleaning doesn’t affect, such as damage or normal wear. A thorough move-out clean can help leave the property ready for inspection, but it is not a guarantee, and we can’t advise on tenancy rules.

What’s the difference between house cleaning and move-out cleaning?

A house clean maintains a home you’re still living in. A move-out clean is done when the property is empty and being handed over, so it goes further — empty rooms mean surfaces normally blocked by furniture get cleaned, and the standard is “ready for the next occupant” rather than “tidy for the week”. Move-out cleans take longer and are priced higher; you can compare both in the estimator on this page. If you’re staying put, house cleaning is the service you want.

How do I get a quote?

Call (250) 879-2425, or send the short request form on this page with your phone, email, the service you need and a quick description of the property. For most standard jobs no in-person visit is needed. A photo is optional and can help confirm scope. We usually respond within an hour.
