Cleaning Services FAQ

What cleaning services does Urgent Clean provide in Kamloops?

Urgent Clean provides residential and property cleaning across Kamloops: house cleaning, deep cleaning, same-day and short-notice cleaning, move-out cleaning, rental turnover cleaning, and post-renovation cleanup. Each service has its own page with the full scope and pricing guidance.

Does Urgent Clean offer same-day cleaning?

Often, yes. Same-day and short-notice appointments may be available depending on the schedule that day, so calling (250) 879-2425 is the fastest way to check. Same-day availability is not guaranteed, but you will get a straight answer quickly either way.

Do you provide rental turnover cleaning for landlords and Airbnb hosts?

Yes. Landlords, property managers and short-term rental hosts can book turnover cleaning between tenants or guests, and several units can be quoted at once. There are no contracts or preferred-provider arrangements in place — anyone can request a quote on the same basis.

How do I request a cleaning quote?

Call (250) 879-2425, or send a short request with your phone, email, the service you need and a description of the property. For most standard jobs no in-person visit is needed, and we usually respond within an hour.
