House Cleaning That Fits Your Schedule

Urgent Clean handles residential cleaning across Kamloops — detached houses, condos, apartments and rental units. Some customers book a single clean to get back on top of things after a busy stretch. Others book regularly so it never gets to that point. Both are normal.

What’s Included in a House Cleaning?

A typical house-cleaning visit covers these four areas. Exact scope is confirmed when we quote, since homes and priorities differ.

Kitchen

  • Countertops wiped down
  • Sink and faucet cleaned
  • Stovetop exterior
  • Appliance exteriors
  • Backsplash
  • Cabinet fronts
  • Accessible surfaces
  • Floors cleaned
  • General waste removal

Bathrooms

  • Toilet cleaned
  • Sink and vanity
  • Mirrors
  • Tub and shower surfaces
  • Fixtures
  • Accessible surfaces
  • Floors cleaned

Bedrooms

  • Dusting of reachable surfaces
  • Mirrors
  • Window sills where accessible
  • Vacuuming
  • Floor cleaning
  • General surface cleaning

Living Areas

  • Dusting of reachable surfaces
  • Tables
  • Mirrors
  • Floors cleaned
  • Vacuuming
  • Hallways and common areas

Detail work such as inside the oven, inside the fridge, or inside cabinets is available as an add-on rather than part of a standard visit — you can price those in the estimator below. If something specific matters to you, mention it in your quote request and we’ll confirm it in the scope.

House Cleaning FAQ

What is included in a standard house cleaning?

A typical visit covers the kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas — wiping reachable surfaces, counters, sinks, fixtures and appliance exteriors, cleaning mirrors, and vacuuming and cleaning floors. Detail work like inside the oven, inside the fridge or inside cabinets is available as an add-on rather than part of a standard visit. The exact scope is confirmed when we quote, since every home is different.

How much does house cleaning cost in Kamloops?

It depends on the size of the home, the number of bathrooms, the condition, the scope and any add-ons. The estimator on this page gives you a ballpark range in a few taps — for example, a 2-bedroom, 1-bathroom standard clean currently estimates at $155–$185. That is an initial range, not a final price: the exact amount is confirmed before anything is booked.

Can I book house cleaning for today or tomorrow?

Often, yes — short-notice work is the core of what Urgent Clean does. Same-day and next-day appointments may be available depending on the schedule that day, so calling (250) 879-2425 is the fastest way to find out. We can’t promise every request, but you’ll get a straight answer quickly either way.

Do you clean apartments and condos?

Yes. Houses, condos, apartments and rental units are all covered. Smaller units generally take less time and are priced accordingly — the estimator on this page starts at studio and one-bedroom sizes.

Can I book a one-time house cleaning?

Yes. One-time cleans are common — before guests arrive, after a busy stretch, or as a seasonal reset. A single clean can be booked on its own; nothing ongoing is required.

Do you offer recurring house cleaning?

Yes, recurring cleaning is one of the services offered. Frequency and scheduling are arranged directly with you when the quote is confirmed, so it fits how often the home actually needs it.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

That’s up to you — some customers stay, others arrange access and get on with their day. Either way, confirm the access arrangements with us when the visit is booked so the team isn’t left waiting at the door.

How long does house cleaning usually take?

It depends on the size of the home, its condition and the scope agreed. A smaller, regularly maintained unit is a much shorter visit than a large home or a first-time deep clean. We’ll give you a realistic time estimate along with the price when the quote is confirmed.

What’s the difference between house cleaning and deep cleaning?

A standard house cleaning keeps an already-maintained home on top of everyday dust and buildup. A deep clean is for homes that need more detailed attention — heavier buildup, neglected areas, or a first-time reset before going back to regular upkeep. Deep cleans take longer and are priced higher; you can compare both ranges in the estimator on this page.

How do I get a quote?

Call (250) 879-2425, or send the short request form on this page with your phone, email, the service you need and a quick description of what needs cleaning. For most standard jobs no in-person visit is needed. A photo is optional and can help confirm pricing. We usually respond within an hour.
