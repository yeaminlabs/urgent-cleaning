What Is Post-Renovation Cleaning?

This guide covers what a post-renovation clean in Kamloops deals with, when to book it, and what moves the price.

Post-renovation cleaning — also called post-construction cleaning — is the clean that makes a finished renovation usable. It is not the same as a builder’s tidy-up. Trades generally clear their tools and sweep the worst of it, which leaves a room that looks finished from the doorway and is still coated in dust everywhere you would actually touch.

The job is mostly about fine particles rather than visible mess. Cutting, sanding and drilling throw dust that stays airborne for hours and then settles gradually onto horizontal surfaces, into the grooves of new trim, along the tops of doors and cabinets, and through the whole floor. That is why a renovation cleanup takes longer than it looks like it should: the dust is in places a normal clean never has to think about.

It matters most on brand new surfaces. Fresh cabinets, counters, tile and fixtures are exactly what the dust lands on, and getting them properly clean before anyone starts using the space is the difference between a renovation that feels finished and one that feels almost finished.

What a Renovation Usually Leaves Behind

Three things account for most of the work, and they are worth understanding before you ask for a quote.

Construction and Drywall Dust

Drywall dust is the defining problem of a renovation cleanup. It is extremely fine, it travels far beyond the room that was worked on, and it does not simply wipe away — dry-dusting tends to move it around and put it back in the air. It also keeps arriving: dust disturbed during the last day of work can still be settling a day or two later, which is the single biggest reason a clean booked too early has to be done twice.

Floors, Baseboards and Trim

Floors take the most traffic and collect everything that falls. New baseboards and trim are worse than they look, because dust gathers along every top edge and in the gap where trim meets wall or floor. Corners, door frames and stair edges are the spots that give away a rushed job, and they are slower to do properly than an open floor.

Cabinets, Fixtures and Surfaces

New cabinets collect dust on top, along the front edges and inside if they were open while work happened. Light fixtures, switch plates, vents, handles and window sills all hold a film that only shows when the light hits them. Kitchens and bathrooms are the two most common renovation cleanups precisely because they combine new surfaces, fine dust and fixtures that need detail work.

Renovation dust also travels. Rooms nowhere near the work often need attention too, so when you ask for a quote it helps to say how far it has spread rather than describing only the room that was renovated.

When Should You Book a Post-Renovation Clean?

The timing rule is simple: after the trades are finished and the materials are gone, not before.

  • After the last work is done. If a contractor is coming back for touch-ups, the clean belongs after that visit, not between the two.
  • After the dust has settled. Leaving a day between the end of work and the clean lets airborne dust land so it can actually be removed.
  • Before furniture arrives. An empty property is the easiest possible condition to clean and the quickest to get through, which also keeps the cost down.
  • Before a handover or move-in. If a date is already set, arrange the clean as soon as you know it. A large cleanup needs more notice than a standard visit.

If your renovation is finishing at the same time as a move, the sequencing matters. Our move-out cleaning checklist covers the handover side of that, and a genuinely tight deadline is worth a phone call rather than a form — though same-day availability is harder to arrange for a big cleanup than for a standard clean.

What Affects the Cleaning Price?

Renovation cleanup starts from $200 for a studio or 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom home with no add-ons. That is the starting configuration, not a typical bill — most renovation cleans are larger than that, and the figure moves with the work involved:

  • Size of the property and how many bedrooms and bathrooms are in scope
  • How far the dust spread — a single renovated room rarely means a single dusty room
  • The condition the work left, since heavy dust and residue take longer than a light film
  • Surface and fixture detail, such as new trim, cabinet edges, vents and light fittings
  • Add-ons such as inside cabinets or appliance interiors, which are common after a kitchen renovation
  • Leftover debris, where general mess is part of the clean and bulk material removal is agreed separately

Post-Renovation Cleaning vs Regular House Cleaning

They share a name and very little else.

Regular house cleaning

Maintaining a lived-in home

Kitchens, bathrooms, floors and surfaces in a home that is already in use. The dirt is everyday dirt, the rooms are furnished, and the visit has a predictable rhythm to it.

Post-renovation cleaning

Making finished work usable

Fine dust on every surface, new fixtures that have never been cleaned, detail work along trim and cabinet edges, and leftover mess from the build. Slower per room, and often across more rooms than were renovated.

A deep clean sits between the two: it goes further than a routine visit, but it is built for accumulated household grime rather than construction dust. If the space has just been worked on, the renovation cleanup is the right scope. You can compare what each visit covers on the cleaning services overview, or read how Urgent Clean handles quoting and scheduling.

Scope note: ordinary renovation dust, sawdust, drywall dust and general leftover mess are part of a cleaning visit. Bulk debris removal is arranged separately, and specialist work such as asbestos, mould remediation or fire and flood restoration is a licensed trade that Urgent Clean does not carry out.

Post-Renovation Cleaning FAQ

What does post-renovation cleaning include?

It is the clean-up once the trades have finished: fine dust lifted off reachable surfaces, floors cleaned throughout, surfaces and fixtures wiped down, kitchen and bathroom areas cleaned, and general leftover mess cleared. New cabinets, counters, trim and fixtures usually need the most attention, because dust settles into every edge and corner while the work is going on. Inside cabinets and appliance interiors can be added when those spaces are empty. Anything needing a trade, such as paint touch-ups or repairs, sits outside a cleaning scope.

How much does post-renovation cleaning cost in Kamloops?

Renovation cleanup starts from $200 for a studio or 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom home with no add-ons. The figure moves with the size of the property, how many rooms the dust reached, the condition the work left things in, and any extras such as inside cabinets or appliance interiors. A single bathroom refresh is a much smaller job than a whole-floor remodel, so the quote is confirmed once the scope is clear rather than guessed in advance.

When should I schedule cleaning after a renovation?

Book it for after the trades are finished and the last materials have gone, not while work is still happening. Renovation dust keeps settling for a day or two after the noise stops, so a clean booked too early usually has to be repeated. If the property is empty of furniture, that is the easiest possible condition to work in and the fastest to get through. Larger cleanups also need more notice than a standard visit, so it is worth arranging as soon as the handover date is set.

Do you remove construction or hazardous materials?

Ordinary renovation dust, sawdust, drywall dust and general leftover mess are part of the job. Bulk construction debris such as offcuts, packaging and unused materials is disposal work rather than cleaning, so it is only included when it has been agreed in advance. Asbestos, mould remediation and fire or flood restoration are specialist trades with their own licensing, and Urgent Clean does not take that work on. If a renovation has turned up something in that category, it needs the right specialist before any cleaning is booked.
