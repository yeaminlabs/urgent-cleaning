Move-Out Cleaning Checklist for Kamloops Renters

Move-out cleaning is a different job from a regular tidy. The unit is empty, so everything that furniture used to hide is suddenly visible — the floor under the bed, the wall behind the couch, the inside of a cupboard nobody opened in two years.

It also happens under a deadline. Move-out cleaning — also called end of tenancy cleaning — has to fit between the last box leaving and the handover, and in Kamloops that window is often a single day. This checklist covers what to work through, room by room, and how to decide whether to do it yourself or book help.

Complete Move-Out Cleaning Checklist

Work through it in this order. Rooms first, floors last — otherwise you clean the floors twice.

Kitchen

The kitchen takes the longest in almost every move-out, so start here while you still have energy.

  • Countertops and backsplash
  • Sink and faucet
  • Stovetop exterior and appliance exteriors
  • Cabinet fronts, and inside cabinets once emptied
  • Reachable surfaces and general waste removal
  • Inside the oven and fridge if the unit is being handed back empty

Bathrooms

Detail-heavy for their size. Small rooms, but a lot of separate surfaces and fixtures.

  • Toilet
  • Sink and vanity
  • Tub and shower surfaces
  • Mirrors and fixtures
  • Accessible surfaces
  • Floors

Bedrooms and Living Areas

These go quickly once they are empty, which is the argument for cleaning after the last load rather than around boxes.

  • Reachable surfaces and general dusting
  • Mirrors
  • Window sills where accessible
  • Tables and shelving where empty and accessible
  • Closet interiors once cleared
  • Hallways and common areas

Floors and Final Details

Last job of the day, once nothing else is going to drop dust on them.

  • Vacuuming throughout
  • Floor cleaning in every room
  • Visible marks and dust on reachable surfaces
  • A general final tidy across the unit
  • Rubbish and leftover items removed

Two things make the whole job shorter. Move your belongings out first — empty rooms clean far better, and you are not working around a pile of boxes. And empty the cabinets before anyone cleans inside them, whether that is you or a cleaner.

Some things sit outside a cleaning scope entirely: repairs, painting, carpet replacement, and anything that needs a trade. Cleaning does not fix damage, and it is worth being clear with yourself about which of the two you are looking at.

What Landlords and Property Managers Look For

Every tenancy is different and the specifics are set by your own agreement, so treat this as general context rather than a list of requirements. What follows is simply what tends to draw attention when an empty unit is walked through.

General cleanliness. Kitchens and bathrooms usually get the closest look, because they are the rooms where buildup is most visible and hardest to miss.

Visible condition. An empty unit shows everything — marks on reachable surfaces, dust along sills and baseboards, floors that were under furniture for years. None of that was visible while you lived there.

Readiness for the next occupant. The practical question is usually whether someone could move in without work being needed first. That is also why belongings and rubbish being fully removed matters as much as the cleaning itself.

Cleaning is one part of handing a place back. Deposit decisions rest with your landlord and the terms of your tenancy agreement, and no cleaning company can speak for that — so be wary of anyone who promises otherwise. What a clean does is remove cleanliness from the list of things in question.

If you are on the other side of this as a landlord, host or property manager, rental turnover cleaning is the service built around the gap between tenancies rather than around a tenant's move date.

DIY Move-Out Cleaning vs Professional Cleaning

Neither is automatically the right answer. It depends on four things.

Doing it yourself

Works when you have the time

  • No cost beyond supplies and your own hours
  • Realistic for a studio or one-bedroom that has been well kept
  • You control the order and can spread it across a few evenings
  • Best when your move date is flexible and not stacked against a handover

Booking a cleaner

Works when the clock is the problem

  • The unit is cleaned in one visit rather than across several evenings
  • Makes more sense as bedrooms and bathrooms multiply
  • Frees moving day for the move itself
  • Useful when possession dates are fixed and cannot slip

Time. Move-out cleaning is slower than people expect, because it is done to an empty-unit standard rather than a lived-in one. The kitchen alone can absorb an afternoon.

Effort. It lands in the same week as packing, loading and address changes. That is usually the deciding factor rather than the cleaning itself.

Property size. A one-bedroom is a manageable evening. A three or four-bedroom house with multiple bathrooms is a different scale of job.

Convenience. If the handover is fixed and the movers are already booked, paying for the cleaning to be handled in a single visit removes the part most likely to overrun.

Move-Out Cleaning FAQ

What is included in move-out cleaning?

A move-out clean covers the unit once it is empty: kitchen surfaces, cabinet fronts and appliance exteriors, bathrooms including toilet, sink, vanity, tub and shower surfaces, reachable surfaces and window sills in bedrooms and living areas, then floors and vacuuming throughout, plus a general final tidy. Inside the oven, fridge and cabinets can be added once those spaces are emptied. Repairs, painting and anything needing a trade sit outside a cleaning scope.

How long does move-out cleaning take?

It depends on the size and condition of the property rather than a fixed number. A small, well-kept one-bedroom is a much shorter job than a three or four-bedroom house with multiple bathrooms, and a unit that has not had a proper clean in years takes longer than one that has. The kitchen is usually the longest single room. We confirm timing along with scope and price when we quote.

When should I schedule move-out cleaning?

Book it for after your belongings are out and before the handover — empty rooms clean far better, and cleaning around boxes means doing parts of it twice. Because that window is often narrow, it is worth arranging the cleaning as soon as your moving date is set rather than in the final few days. Short-notice bookings around a move date are common, though same-day availability depends on the schedule and is not guaranteed.

Do you clean rental properties?

Yes. Move-out cleaning is available for renters finishing a tenancy, and rental turnover cleaning between tenants or guests is a core part of the work. Landlords, property managers and short-term rental hosts can request quotes directly, including for several units at once.
