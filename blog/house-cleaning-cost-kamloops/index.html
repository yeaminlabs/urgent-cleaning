What Affects House Cleaning Prices?

Four things move the number more than anything else.

Size of Your Home

Size is the starting point for every quote. More bedrooms and bathrooms mean more surfaces, more floor area and more time on site, so a studio or one-bedroom condo sits at the bottom of the range and a four or five-bedroom house sits at the top.

Bathrooms carry more weight than their square footage suggests. They are detail-heavy rooms — tub and shower surfaces, fixtures, mirrors, toilet and vanity — so a two-bathroom home takes noticeably longer than a one-bathroom home of the same size.

Regular vs Deep Cleaning

A regular or standard clean assumes a home that is already reasonably maintained. It covers the everyday work: kitchen and bathroom surfaces, dusting, floors and common areas.

A deep clean goes further, into buildup that a weekly tidy never reaches, and it costs more because it takes longer. Deep cleaning is the right call when a place has been neglected or overdue, before or after guests, or when you want a full reset rather than upkeep. If you are unsure which one you need, describe the property and we will tell you.

Move-In and Move-Out Cleaning

End-of-tenancy work is priced above a standard clean. A move-out clean is done on an empty unit and is held to an inspection standard rather than a lived-in one, which means more time in the places that matter to a landlord or property manager.

Move-in cleaning works the same way in reverse — starting fresh in a new place before your things arrive. Landlords and hosts turning a unit between occupants usually want rental turnover cleaning, which is built around a tight window between tenancies.

Frequency of Cleaning

How often a home is cleaned changes how much work each visit takes. A property on a regular schedule stays on top of everyday dust and buildup, so each clean is lighter than a one-off visit to a home that has gone a long time without one.

The first clean on a home that is overdue is often the largest, and later visits settle down from there. There is no contract and nothing to sign up for — you can book a single visit and come back whenever you need one.
