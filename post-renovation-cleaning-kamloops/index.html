What Is Post-Renovation Cleaning?

Renovations leave dust behind. Even when the trades have swept up and taken their tools, fine particles from cutting, sanding and drywall work end up on every surface in the space — and on plenty of surfaces outside it.

Post-renovation cleaning is the pass that comes after the building work is done: removing that visible dust from reachable surfaces, cleaning floors, wiping down the new finishes, and clearing general leftover mess so the room is actually usable rather than just finished.

It’s a different job from a regular clean. There’s more dust, it’s finer, and it sits on surfaces that have never been cleaned before. What’s covered is agreed with you when we quote — renovations vary enormously in what they leave behind, so the scope is set against your actual project rather than a standard checklist.

Renovation Cleanup Checklist

Not what we clean — what to sort out first. Renovation cleanups go wrong more often from bad timing than from the cleaning itself.

Before booking

  • Make sure the major construction work is genuinely finished — cleaning before the last dusty job is wasted money
  • Remove large leftover materials, offcuts and packaging
  • Have any specialist disposal handled separately first
  • Check whether dust is still actively settling; a day or two’s wait often gives a better result
  • Confirm power, water and lighting are working in the space

Tell us when you request the quote

  • What the renovation involved, and which rooms it touched
  • How far the dust has spread beyond the work area
  • The size of the property and how much of it needs cleaning
  • Your priority areas and the date it needs to be ready
  • Anything unusual left behind, so the scope is realistic
  • Photos — dust levels are far easier to show than describe

Photos are more useful on this service than any other. A quick picture of a dusty room tells us more in a second than a paragraph of description, and it makes the quote much more likely to match the job.

Post-Renovation Cleaning FAQ

What is post-renovation cleaning?

Post-renovation cleaning is the clean-up after building work is finished. Even when the trades have tidied up, renovations leave fine dust that settles on every surface — and keeps settling for a while afterwards. This service removes that visible dust and leftover mess from reachable surfaces so the space is ready to use, within the cleaning scope agreed when we quote.

What does renovation cleanup include?

A post-renovation clean may include removing visible dust from reachable surfaces, cleaning floors throughout, wiping down surfaces, cleaning kitchen and bathroom areas, and removing general leftover mess within the agreed cleaning scope. The exact scope is confirmed when we quote, because renovations vary enormously in what they leave behind.

Do you clean after kitchen or bathroom renovations?

Yes — those are the two most common renovation cleanups. Kitchen and bathroom work tends to generate the most fine dust and leave the most residue on new surfaces, fixtures and floors. Tell us what was done and we will build the scope around it.

Do you remove construction debris?

Large construction debris or specialized waste removal is outside the normal cleaning scope unless specifically agreed. General leftover mess is included, but material such as offcuts, packaging, old fixtures or drywall sheets should be cleared before the visit, or discussed in advance so the quote reflects it. We do not handle hazardous materials, asbestos or demolition waste.

How much does post-renovation cleaning cost in Kamloops?

The cost depends on the size of the property, its condition, the scope of the renovation and the cleaning requirements. Use our estimator for a quick ballpark price, or request a quote for your specific property.

Can contractors request cleanup quotes?

Yes. Contractors, builders and renovation companies are welcome to request quotes for final clean-up before handover, on the same basis as homeowners. There are no existing contracts or preferred-contractor arrangements in place with any company.

How soon can renovation cleanup be scheduled?

It depends on availability and the size of the job. Short-notice requests are welcome and same-day appointments may be available depending on the schedule, though a large post-renovation clean usually needs a longer window than a routine visit. Calling (250) 879-2425 is the fastest way to check.

Do you clean newly renovated homes before moving in?

Yes, and it is a common reason to book. If the work is finished and you want the place cleaned before furniture and belongings arrive, an empty property is the easiest possible condition to clean properly.

Do you clean dust after renovations?

Yes, that is the main part of the job. Renovation dust is fine and settles gradually, so it can reappear on surfaces for a day or two after the work stops. If dust is still actively settling it is usually worth waiting until things have properly settled before booking, otherwise the same surfaces need doing twice.

How do I request a quote?

A photo is optional but especially useful here — dust levels are much easier to show than to describe. We usually respond within an hour.
