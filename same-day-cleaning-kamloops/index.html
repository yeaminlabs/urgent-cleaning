That’s the situation Urgent Clean is set up for. Tell us what needs cleaning and roughly when, and you’ll get a quote back quickly — usually within an hour — along with a straight answer on whether we can be there today or tomorrow.

What Same-Day Cleaning Actually Means

Worth being straight about this, because the phrase gets used loosely.

What it does mean

A fast answer, and often a fast booking

  • We check the day’s schedule as soon as you call
  • You get a quote quickly — usually within the hour
  • No separate site visit needed for most standard homes
  • If today is full, we’ll tell you what is open — often the next day

What it doesn’t mean

Not a guarantee, and we won’t pretend otherwise

  • Same-day slots depend entirely on what’s already booked
  • Busy days fill up, and we can’t take every request
  • Large or heavily soiled properties usually need more notice
  • We don’t do biohazard, flood or fire restoration work

If we can’t fit you in, you’ll hear that immediately rather than after a day of waiting on a callback. When you’re on a deadline, a fast no is worth almost as much as a yes.

Same-Day Cleaning FAQ

Can I book same-day cleaning in Kamloops?

Often, yes — but it depends on the day. Same-day appointments may be available depending on the schedule, so calling (250) 879-2425 is the fastest way to find out. If there is a slot that works we will confirm it on the call; if there is not, you will know straight away rather than waiting on a callback.

What types of cleaning can be done the same day?

Standard residential cleaning is the most realistic same-day job — kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas in houses, condos and apartments. Larger jobs such as a full deep clean or a post-renovation cleanup usually need more time and more notice. Depending on the size and condition of the space, we may suggest focusing on priority rooms first.

How quickly can I get a quote?

Usually within an hour. For most standard properties we can quote from a description, so nothing waits on a separate site visit. If you are working to a deadline today, calling is faster than the form.

How much does same-day cleaning cost?

The cost depends on factors such as the size of the property, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, condition, and whether additional detail cleaning is requested. Use our estimator for a quick ballpark price, or request a quote for your specific property.

Do you clean apartments and houses?

Yes — houses, condos, apartments, townhouses and rental units across Kamloops. Smaller units generally take less time and are priced accordingly; the estimator on this page starts at studio and one-bedroom sizes.

Can I request short-notice cleaning?

Yes, and it is worth asking even when same-day is full. Next-day and later-in-the-week bookings are often easier to place than same-day. Tell us the deadline you are working to and we will tell you what is realistic.

Do I need to be home?

That is up to you — some customers stay, others arrange access and get on with their day. Either way, confirm the access arrangements such as keys, a buzzer code or someone on site when the visit is booked, so the team is not left waiting at the door.

How do I request availability?

Call (250) 879-2425 for the fastest answer, or send the short form on this page with your phone, email, the service you need and a quick description of what needs cleaning. We usually respond within an hour.

Is same-day cleaning guaranteed?

No. Same-day availability depends on the schedule that day and cannot be guaranteed. We will always tell you quickly whether we can fit you in, and if we cannot, we will say so rather than leaving you waiting.

What information helps you provide a faster quote?

The size of the place in bedrooms and bathrooms, what kind of clean you need, the deadline it has to be done by, and anything unusual about the condition or access. A photo is optional but can settle questions faster than a description. The more accurate the detail, the more accurate the quote.
