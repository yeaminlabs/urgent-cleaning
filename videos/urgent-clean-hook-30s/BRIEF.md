## Intent

A 30-second vertical promo that stops the scroll in the first two seconds with a
deadline hook a landlord recognises ("tenant moved out this morning, new one
moves in tomorrow"), then a second hook for older homeowners who no longer want
to do the heavy scrubbing themselves. Silent-autoplay friendly: every line is on
screen, no voiceover.

- assets/img/*.webp — real Urgent Clean job photos copied from the site's assets/work/.
- assets/img/logo.png — light-on-dark Urgent Clean logo.
- assets/fonts/ — Bricolage Grotesque (brand face, DESIGN.md) + JetBrains Mono for numbers.
