Contact FAQ

How do I request a cleaning quote?

Two ways. Call (250) 879-2425 for the fastest answer, or fill in the form on this page with your phone, email, the service you need and a short description of the property. A photo is optional and often settles questions faster than a description. For most standard properties no in-person visit is needed, and we usually reply within an hour with scope, timing and price.

What cleaning services does Urgent Clean provide?

House cleaning, deep cleaning, move-out and move-in cleaning, rental turnover cleaning, post-renovation cleanup, and same-day or short-notice cleaning. All of it is residential and rental property cleaning — houses, condos, apartments and rental units. We do not offer commercial or office cleaning, and we do not take on hazardous material, asbestos or demolition work.

Does Urgent Clean offer same-day cleaning?

Often, yes. Same-day and short-notice appointments may be available depending on the schedule that day, so calling (250) 879-2425 is the fastest way to check. Same-day availability is not guaranteed, but you will get a straight answer quickly either way.

Do you clean rental properties?

Yes. Rental turnover cleaning between tenants or guests is a core part of the work, and move-out cleaning is available for renters finishing a tenancy. Landlords, property managers and short-term rental hosts can request quotes directly, including for several units at once.
