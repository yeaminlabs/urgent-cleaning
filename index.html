Common questions about cleaning in Kamloops

Everything landlords and homeowners ask before they call.

Do you offer same-day cleaning in Kamloops?

Yes — same-day cleaning is often possible in Kamloops, scheduling permitting. Call or send a quick request before noon and we can frequently have a team at your property that same afternoon; evening slots are sometimes available too. Reaching out as early as possible gives you the best chance at short notice.

How much does house cleaning in Kamloops cost?

Prices depend on bedrooms, bathrooms, and the condition of the property. A standard 2-bedroom clean typically runs $155–$185. A move-out clean of the same unit runs $225–$270. Use the price estimator on this page for a live range, then call or send a quick request to confirm — most standard jobs don't need an in-person site visit.

What areas of Kamloops do you serve?

We cover all of Kamloops: Downtown, Sahali, Brocklehurst, Aberdeen, Westsyde, Valleyview, Juniper Ridge, Pineview, Rayleigh, Sun Rivers, Dallas, and Barnhartvale. Not on this list? Call us — we regularly take jobs in surrounding areas not listed here.

What's included in a move-out clean in Kamloops?

Our move-out cleaning in Kamloops covers every room top to bottom: floors, surfaces, baseboards, light fixtures, window sills, and all bathrooms scrubbed. Detailed extras like inside appliances (fridge, oven) and inside cabinets can be included in your quote based on the scope you need. The goal is inspection-ready — the property in the best possible shape for your landlord's walkthrough.

Do you do post-renovation cleaning in Kamloops?

Yes. We remove drywall dust, paint overspray, adhesive residue, and construction debris from surfaces, floors, and fixtures so the space is ready to move into or back to renting. The amount of cleaning needed varies with the scope of the renovation — we confirm the details and pricing before we start.

Do I need to sign a contract?

No contracts. No recurring commitments unless you want them. You call when you need us, we quote on the phone, we show up. For landlords who want a regular turnover schedule, we can set that up — but it's never a requirement.
