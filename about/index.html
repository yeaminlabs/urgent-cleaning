A Local Kamloops Cleaning Company

Urgent Clean is a Kamloops cleaning company, working in this city and the areas around it. Locally operating in Kamloops since 2025, we clean houses, condos, apartments and rental units for the people who live in them and the people who own them.

Working here means knowing how the local market actually behaves. Rental turnovers land in tight windows between tenancies. Showings get booked with little warning. Renovations finish late and the handover date does not move. Those are the situations people call us about, and the service is built around them rather than a fixed weekly route.

That shapes how we respond. Call or send a short request and you will normally hear back within an hour with scope, timing and price — and for most standard properties there is no separate site visit to wait on first. If we can fit you in, you will know quickly. If we cannot, you will know that just as quickly, which matters when you are working to a deadline.

The rest is straightforward: agree what is being cleaned before the visit, then do that. No contracts, nothing to sign up for, and a scope you approved rather than a generic checklist.

About Urgent Clean FAQ

What cleaning services does Urgent Clean provide in Kamloops?

House cleaning, deep cleaning, move-out and move-in cleaning, rental turnover cleaning, post-renovation cleanup, and same-day or short-notice cleaning. All of it is residential and rental property cleaning — houses, condos, apartments and rental units. Each service has its own page with the full scope and pricing guidance.

Is Urgent Clean available for same-day cleaning?

Often, yes. Same-day and short-notice appointments may be available depending on the schedule that day, so calling (250) 879-2425 is the fastest way to check. Same-day availability is not guaranteed, but you will get a straight answer quickly either way.

Does Urgent Clean clean rental properties?

Yes. Rental turnover cleaning between tenants or guests is a core part of the work, and move-out cleaning is available for renters finishing a tenancy. Landlords, property managers and short-term rental hosts can request quotes directly, including for several units at once.

How can I request a cleaning quote?

Call (250) 879-2425, or send a short request with your phone, email, the service you need and a description of the property. For most standard jobs no in-person visit is needed, and we usually respond within an hour.
