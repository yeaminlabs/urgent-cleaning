What Is Deep Cleaning?

Deep cleaning is a more detailed cleaning service for homes that need extra attention compared with regular maintenance cleaning. Same rooms, same surfaces — just considerably more time spent on each of them.

It’s the right call when a place hasn’t been cleaned in a while, when buildup has collected in the spots a routine visit passes over, or when you simply want the home reset properly rather than tidied. People book it seasonally, before guests arrive, after a stretch of neglect, or as a one-time starting point before moving to regular cleaning.

What it isn’t: an unlimited clean. A deep clean covers an agreed scope, and we’ll be straightforward with you about what fits the time and budget you have.

Deep Cleaning or Regular Cleaning?

Neither replaces the other — most homes want both at different times. A deep clean resets the place; regular cleaning keeps it that way.

Regular cleaning

Keeping on top of it

For a home that’s already being maintained.

  • Routine upkeep between visits
  • Everyday dust and surface mess
  • The surfaces you use most
  • Ongoing, on whatever schedule suits

Deep cleaning

Resetting it properly

For a home that needs more than routine attention.

  • More time spent on detail
  • Built-up grime on reachable surfaces
  • Additional areas a routine visit doesn’t reach
  • Occasional — seasonal, or a one-time reset

A common pattern: one deep clean to get the place back to where you want it, then regular cleaning to hold it there. If you’re not sure which you need, describe the situation when you call and we’ll tell you honestly.

Deep Cleaning Checklist

Not what we clean — what helps the visit go well. A deep clean is time-limited work, so anything that saves time gets spent on cleaning instead.

Before we arrive

  • Clear clutter off surfaces where you can — clear counters clean far better
  • Put away anything you’d rather we didn’t move
  • Empty the cabinets or fridge if you’ve asked for those as add-ons
  • Make sure we can get in — keys, buzzer code or someone on site
  • Confirm the power and water are on

Tell us when you request the quote

  • Bedrooms and bathrooms, and roughly how big the place is
  • Your priority areas — what you most want done properly
  • Any heavy buildup or problem spots, so the quote is realistic
  • How long it’s been since the last thorough clean
  • Whether you want the oven, fridge or cabinets included
  • A photo or two if something is easier to show than describe

Being specific about problem areas is the single most useful thing you can do — it’s the difference between an accurate quote and a scope that changes on the day.

Deep Cleaning FAQ

What is included in a deep cleaning?

A deep clean covers the same rooms as a regular clean — kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas — but with more time spent on detail: built-up grime on reachable surfaces, fixtures, appliance exteriors, backsplashes, cabinet fronts, and floors throughout. The exact scope is agreed when we quote, so you can tell us which areas matter most. Detail work such as inside the oven, inside the fridge and inside cabinets is priced separately as an add-on.

How is deep cleaning different from regular cleaning?

Regular cleaning keeps a home that is already being maintained on top of everyday dust and mess. A deep clean is for when things have built up — it takes longer, covers more detail, and reaches areas a routine visit does not usually get to. Many people book one deep clean and then move to regular cleaning to keep the place that way.

How much does deep cleaning cost in Kamloops?

The cost depends on factors such as the size of the property, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, condition, and whether additional detail cleaning is requested. Use our estimator for a quick ballpark price, or request a quote for your specific property.

How long does deep cleaning take?

It depends on the size of the property, its condition and the scope you have requested. A deep clean takes noticeably longer than a routine visit to the same home. We will give you a realistic time estimate along with the price when the quote is confirmed.

How often should a home be deep cleaned?

There is no single right answer — it depends on the household, how much use the place gets and how often it is cleaned in between. Many people book one seasonally, or once or twice a year, with regular cleaning in between. If you are not sure, describe the situation when you call and we will give you an honest view.

Can I book deep cleaning on short notice?

Sometimes. A deep clean needs a longer window than a routine visit, so it is harder to fit in at short notice than a standard clean. Short-notice requests are still welcome — call (250) 879-2425 and we will tell you quickly what is realistic. Same-day availability depends on the schedule and is not guaranteed.

Do you clean kitchens and bathrooms during deep cleaning?

Yes — they are usually where most of the time goes. Kitchens and bathrooms collect the most buildup, so a deep clean spends longer on surfaces, fixtures, tiles and appliance exteriors in those rooms than a routine visit would.

Do you clean apartments and condos?

Yes — houses, condos, apartments, townhouses and rental units across Kamloops. Smaller units generally take less time and are priced accordingly; the estimator on this page starts at studio and one-bedroom sizes.

Can I request specific areas?

Yes, and it helps. If particular rooms or problem areas matter most to you, say so when you request the quote and we will build the scope around them. That is especially useful when time or budget is limited — we would rather do the priority areas properly than spread the same hours thinly across everything.

How do I get a deep cleaning quote?

Call (250) 879-2425, or send the short request form on this page with your phone, email, the service you need and a description of what needs attention. For most standard properties no in-person visit is needed. A photo is optional and can help confirm scope. We usually respond within an hour.
