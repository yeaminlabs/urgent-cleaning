What if I told you the CIA once experimented on Americans without their consent?
In 1953, the CIA launched MKUltra. A secret program exploring drugs, hypnosis, and techniques to influence human behavior.
Some subjects were given LSD without knowing it. The experiments took place across universities, hospitals, and other institutions.
In 1973, many MKUltra records were destroyed. Years later, investigations exposed parts of what happened.
The terrifying part? We may never know the full extent of the experiments.
