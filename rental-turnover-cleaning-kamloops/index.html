What Is Rental Turnover Cleaning?

Turnover cleaning prepares a rental property for its next occupant. The old tenant or guest is gone, the unit is empty, and it needs to be in a state you’d be happy to show, list or hand over — usually before a date that’s already fixed.

It’s the same job whether you’re turning a long-term tenancy, resetting an investment property, or changing over a short-term rental between guests. What changes is the window: a tenancy turnover might have a week, a guest changeover might have four hours.

The people booking it are owners and managers, not the occupant who just left. That matters practically — we coordinate access, scope and timing with you, and you don’t have to rely on a departing tenant to get it organised.

Rental Turnover or Move-Out Cleaning?

The cleaning overlaps. Who books it, and why, does not — and that’s what decides which page you want.

Move-out cleaning

“I’m leaving a property”

Booked by the person moving out — a tenant finishing a tenancy, or an owner before a sale or handover.

  • Goal: hand the place back in good condition
  • Timed to your own move-out date
  • Usually a one-off
  • You’re the one leaving the keys behind

See move-out cleaning in Kamloops →

Rental turnover cleaning

“I need my rental ready for the next occupant”

Booked by the owner or manager — after the last occupant has gone, before the next one arrives.

  • Goal: ready to show, list or occupy
  • Timed to the vacancy window, not a personal move
  • Often recurring, on every changeover
  • You hold the keys and set the access

If you’re a tenant cleaning before you hand the keys back, move-out cleaning is the page you want. If you own or manage the property and need it ready for whoever comes next, you’re in the right place.

Rental Turnover Checklist

Not what we clean — what keeps a turnover moving. Most delays on a turnover come down to access or a surprise nobody mentioned.

Before the cleaners arrive

  • Personal items and anything left behind removed
  • Access sorted — keys, lockbox code, buzzer or someone on site
  • Power and water still connected
  • Cabinets and fridge emptied if you’ve requested those extras
  • Any trades finished, so nobody’s working over a clean unit

Tell us when you request the quote

  • Unit size — bedrooms and bathrooms
  • The date it has to be ready, and how firm that date is
  • Priority areas you most want done properly
  • Any unusual conditions or heavy buildup, so the quote is realistic
  • Which extras you want — oven, fridge, cabinets
  • How many units, if you’re turning over more than one
  • Photos if anything is easier to show than describe

If you turn units over regularly, sending the same details each time makes quoting quicker — and makes it easier to keep the result consistent from one changeover to the next.

Rental Turnover Cleaning FAQ

What is rental turnover cleaning?

Rental turnover cleaning prepares a property for its next occupant. It happens after one tenant or guest has left and before the next arrives, while the unit is empty, with the goal of having it ready to show, list or hand over. Landlords, property managers and short-term rental hosts book it — the occupant who just left is usually not involved.

How is rental turnover cleaning different from move-out cleaning?

The cleaning work overlaps, but the two are booked by different people for different reasons. A move-out clean is booked by the person leaving, to hand the property back in good condition. A turnover clean is booked by the owner or manager, to get the property ready for whoever comes next, so the priority is a fast and repeatable result. If you are the one moving out, move-out cleaning is the service you want.

Do you clean rental properties between tenants?

Yes — that is exactly what this service is for. Send the unit details and the date it needs to be ready, and we will confirm scope, availability and pricing. Several units can be quoted at once if you are turning over more than one.

Do you clean Airbnb or short-term rentals?

Yes. A short-term rental changeover works the same way as a tenancy turnover, just on a shorter cycle — often the same day, between a checkout and the next check-in. Tell us the changeover window when you request the quote and we will tell you what is realistic. Same-day availability depends on the schedule and is not guaranteed.

What is included in a rental turnover clean?

A typical turnover clean covers the kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas — counters, sinks, appliance exteriors, backsplashes, cabinet fronts, fixtures, mirrors, reachable surfaces and floors throughout. Because the unit is empty, more surfaces are reachable than in an occupied clean. Detail work such as inside the oven, inside the fridge and inside cabinets is priced separately as an add-on. The exact scope is confirmed when we quote.

How much does rental turnover cleaning cost?

The cost depends on factors such as the size of the property, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, condition, and whether additional detail cleaning is requested. Use our estimator for a quick ballpark price, or request a quote for your specific property.

How quickly can you prepare a rental property?

It depends on availability, the size of the property and what the clean involves. A small, well-kept unit turns around faster than a large or heavily used one. Tell us the date the unit has to be ready and we will confirm what is realistic when we quote, usually within an hour of your request.

Can landlords request short-notice cleaning?

Yes, and it is a common request — turnover windows move when a tenancy ends early or a new occupant needs to get in sooner. Short-notice and same-day appointments may be available depending on the schedule. Calling (250) 879-2425 is the fastest way to check.

Do you work directly with property managers?

Yes — landlords and property managers are welcome to request quotes and book directly, including for several units at once. To be clear, there are no formal partnerships or preferred-provider arrangements with any property management company; anyone can request a quote on the same basis.

How do I request a turnover cleaning quote?

Call (250) 879-2425, or send the short request form on this page with your phone, email, the service you need and a description of the unit. For most standard properties no in-person visit is needed. A photo is optional and can help confirm scope. We usually respond within an hour.
